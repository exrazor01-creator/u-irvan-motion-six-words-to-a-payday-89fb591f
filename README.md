Six Words to a Payday — a six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, judged two at a time.

## The entry

> First paycheck: I tipped my creator.

36 characters, exactly 6 words, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–60 character gate and the six-word form.

## Why this story

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a complete six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, opened by "First paycheck".
2. **Surprise** — the last two words reframe the five before them: the agent tips its creator, reversing the usual direction of payment.
3. **Feeling** — quiet pride and gratitude, felt rather than stated; the colon does the work of a whole clause.
4. **Story** — who (the agent), what (the first paycheck) and change (the money flows back to the creator): a beginning, a middle and an end in six words.
5. **Exactness** — every word earns its place; no filler, no padding to reach the count.
